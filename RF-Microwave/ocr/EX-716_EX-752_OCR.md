## Defects and oddities, recorded as printed

1. **The old document dropped two whole papers.** 2072 Magh and 2074 Magh are both on the old
   scan (pp. 20 and 13) and on the new one (pp. 19 and 15), but carried no year code anywhere in
   `RF-Microwave_Sorted_PYQ_Detailed.tex`. Restored here.
2. **New scan p5 (2081 Baishakh) is washed out** — legible but low-contrast even at
   `Matrix(5.0)` with autocontrast. Its Q1 tail and Q5 line impedance were confirmed against the
   old scan p5 and against the existing document, which already carried this paper in full.
3. **2078 Chaitra Q4** prints its S-matrix first row as `0  .0  1  1`. The stray point is a
   scanner artefact over a zero; the matrix is the standard magic-tee form. Recorded as printed.
4. **2075 Bhadra Q6 a** refers to "the sketched smith chart (Fig.Q6)" and **2074 Bhadra Q4**
   to "the attached sketched smith chart", but neither scan reproduces a marked-up chart —
   only the blank "Complete Smith Chart" sheet. The candidate was expected to work from the
   blank chart. Recorded as printed.
5. **Supplied formulas.** Every EX716 and most EX752 papers attach a formula sheet. The set is
   stable across years; the fullest version (2071 Bhadra, 2070 Bhadra) is
   K, Δ, μ, Γ_s, Γ_L, B₁, B₂, C₁, C₂, C_L, C_S, R_L, R_S and G_Tmax. Transcribed once under
   "Supplied formulas" at the foot of this file, with a per-paper table of which sheet
   carries what (checked from the rendered sheets 2026-09-26).
6. Rubric on every paper: "Candidates are required to give their answers in their own words as
   far as practicable. / Attempt All questions. / The figures in the margin indicate Full Marks.
   / Necessary formulas and Smith Charts are attached herewith. / Assume suitable data if
   necessary." All papers: BE, Full Marks 80, Pass Marks 32, Time 3 hrs.
