\section{RF and M/W Transmission Lines: Numerical Problems}
{\color{sub}\footnotesize 24 solved PYQ constructions \gap$\cdot$\gap
\mk{$\approx$230 marks} across 24 papers \gap$\cdot$\gap
1 chart reading, 6 single-stub, 17 double-stub}

\vspace{3pt}\noindent
\colorbox{gdL}{\makebox[\dimexpr\textwidth-2\fboxsep][l]{%
  \textcolor{gd}{\bfseries\footnotesize READ THIS FIRST}}}
\par\nobreak\vspace{3pt}

Every problem below is worked \textbf{as a Smith chart construction}, step by step ---
which circle to draw, which arc to follow, what to read off the rim --- because that is
what the examiner marks. The numbers were computed exactly from the transmission-line
equations, so they are what a perfectly drawn chart would give; your hand-drawn readings
will differ in the second decimal place and that is fine.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Two solutions are normal.} Single-stub and double-stub tuners each have
    two valid answers. Quote either --- but say which one you chose, and why.
  \item \textbf{Always check the forbidden region} before starting a double stub:
    $g_1 \le 1/\sin^2\beta d$. Stating this earns marks even when the load passes.
  \item \textbf{Never drop the S-matrix rider} --- \S2.8, two lines, 2--6 marks.
\end{itemize}

\T{M. The Three Methods, in Brief}

\sbs{%
\lead{M1 --- Reading the chart (Problem 1)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Normalise $z_L = Z_L/Z_0$ and plot.
  \item Swing the \textbf{SWR circle} about the centre through that point.
    $|\Gamma_L|$ is its radius; $S$ is where it cuts the right-hand real axis.
  \item Moving along the line is \textbf{rotation at constant radius}: clockwise toward the
    generator, one lap $=\lambda/2$. Read distances on the WTG rim scale.
  \item The circle crosses the horizontal axis twice per lap --- those are the only
    \textbf{purely resistive} points, at $V_{\max}$ ($r=S$) and $V_{\min}$ ($r=1/S$).
\end{enumerate}
\lead{M2 --- Single-stub shunt tuner (Problems 2--7)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Plot $z_L$, half-turn to $y_L$ (shunt stubs \emph{add susceptance}, so all
    stub work is done in admittance).
  \item Rotate clockwise to the \textbf{$g=1$ circle}. It is cut \textbf{twice}
    $\rightarrow$ $d_1$, $d_2$.
  \item There $y = 1 \pm jb$. The stub must supply $\mp jb$.
  \item For the length, start at the stub's own termination --- \textbf{open} stub at
    $y=0$, \textbf{short} stub at $y=\infty$ --- and run clockwise round the rim to that
    susceptance. $\ell_{short} = \ell_{open} \pm \lambda/4$.
\end{enumerate}}{%
\lead{M3 --- Double-stub shunt tuner (Problems 8--24)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Plot $z_L$, half-turn to $y_L$; if the first stub is offset, rotate clockwise to
    get $y_1$, the admittance \textbf{just before stub 1}.
  \item \textbf{Check $g_1 \le 1/\sin^2\beta d$.} If it fails, no length of stub can match
    this load at this spacing.
  \item Draw the \textbf{spacing circle}: the $g=1$ circle rotated the stub spacing $d$
    \emph{toward the load}.
  \item Stub 1 can only slide the point \textbf{along its own constant-$g$ circle}. Follow
    that circle to its two intersections with the spacing circle $\rightarrow$
    $y_a$, $y_a'$. Then $b_1 = \operatorname{Im}(y_a) - \operatorname{Im}(y_1)$.
  \item Rotate $y_a$ clockwise by $d$ on a new SWR circle. It lands on $g=1$ at
    $y_2 = 1 + jb$.
  \item Stub 2 supplies $-jb$. Read both lengths off the rim as in M2.
\end{enumerate}
\lead{Sign conventions that decide the answer}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Clockwise $=$ toward the generator.} One full lap is $\lambda/2$, so a
    quarter turn is $\lambda/8$ and $3\lambda/8$ is three quarter-turns.
  \item On the \textbf{admittance} chart the terminations swap ends: $y=0$ (open) is at
    the left, $y=\infty$ (short) at the right --- the mirror of the impedance chart.
    Getting this backwards corrupts every stub length by $\lambda/4$.
  \item Upper half $= +b =$ \textbf{capacitive}; lower half $= -b =$ inductive.
\end{itemize}
\lead{Analytic back-check}
\[ B = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{g_1(1+t^2) - g_1^2 t^2}}{t}, \quad t = \tan\beta d,
   \quad b_1 = B - b_L \]
{\footnotesize\color{sub} Note the leading $1$ in the numerator, not $g_1$.}}
