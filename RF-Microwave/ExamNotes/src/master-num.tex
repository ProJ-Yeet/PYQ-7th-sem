\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.88\textwidth}\small\color{sub}
\mk{Every answer in this volume is machine checked.} \code{src/rf.py} solves the
transmission line and both stub tuners exactly, with no chart read and no formula quoted
from memory. \code{src/verify.py} then walks each \textbf{published} design forward through
the line equations and asserts $y_{in} = 1 + j0$, re-deriving every susceptance from the
\textbf{published stub length}, so a wrong length cannot hide behind a right $b$.
\code{src/sparam.py} re-derives each device matrix from its stated properties rather than
quoting it, brute-forces the three-port impossibility theorem, and reduces an $n$-port by
terminating ports. \code{src/wg.py} tests every printed waveguide field component against
both curl equations and the walls, and is mutation tested. \code{src/amp.py} and
\code{src/filt.py} reproduce the deck's worked amplifier and filter examples and sample the
stability circles. \code{src/ant.py} asserts every radiation-zone number, including the
cases where the standard formulas are \textbf{not valid}. \code{src/smith.py} draws each
chart as a \textbf{construction} and asserts its own geometry before rendering.
\end{minipage}
