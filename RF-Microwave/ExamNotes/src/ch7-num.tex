Chapter 7 has one calculation: where the three radiation zones of an antenna begin and end. It
is always a 3 or 4 mark part of a longer theory question.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The procedure}: (1) $\lambda = c/f$, (2) check $D > \lambda$, (3)
    $R_1 = 0.62\sqrt{D^3/\lambda}$, (4) $R_2 = 2D^2/\lambda$, (5) state the three zones and
    which are hazardous. Problem 1 shows every line.
  \item \textbf{Do step 2.} When the antenna is smaller than a wavelength (82 Ba's
    quarter-wave antennas) the formulas put the ``far field'' inside the reactive field. Give
    the formula result, then the small-antenna boundaries $\lambda/2\pi$ and $2\lambda$
    (Problem 2).
  \item \textbf{Units}: keep $\lambda$ and $D$ in metres; the answers come out in metres.
  \item Every number below is asserted in \code{src/ant.py}, which also cross-checks the
    81 Bh zone answer against an ICNIRP compliance distance.
\end{itemize}
