\mk{Three clashes to watch}:
\textbf{FCC} is the \emph{forward control channel} throughout these notes; the US regulator is
always written out as the Federal Communications Commission, and \textbf{FCCH} is GSM's
frequency correction channel, a different thing again. \textbf{BCH} is the
Bose--Chaudhuri--Hocquenghem block code in \textbf{Ch 6} and GSM's \emph{broadcast channel} in
\textbf{Ch 8}. \textbf{SCH} is GSM's synchronisation channel, never a sub-channel. \\
