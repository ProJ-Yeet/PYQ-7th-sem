\begin{minipage}{0.90\textwidth}\small\color{sub}
\mk{Every answer in this volume is machine checked.}
\code{src/verify.py} recomputes each published result independently from the problem
statement and asserts it against the printed value $-$ convolutions and correlations,
ROC-aware inverse Z-transforms, pole-zero and magnitude responses, the lattice step-down
in \textbf{exact rational arithmetic} (\code{lattice.py}) and the quantization figures
(\code{quant.py}), every window value, filter length, Kaiser $\beta$ and Bessel figure
(\code{fir.py}), every order, cut-off, pole, $H(s)$ and $H(z)$ (\code{iir.py}, itself
self-tested against Oppenheim examples 7.2, 7.3 and 7.6), and every DFT, butterfly stage
and circular convolution (\code{dft.py}, which checks DIT and DIF against the direct DFT
on nine sequences before it prints). \textbf{1743 checks, all passing.}
\end{minipage}
